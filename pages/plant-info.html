Monstera Deliciosa (Swiss Cheese Plant)

A vigorous flowering evergreen native to tropical rainforests of Central and South America. In its natural jungle habitat, it climbs tall tree trunks via aerial roots to reach filtered canopy light. Its characteristic perforated (fenestrated) leaves evolved to withstand high rainforest winds and maximize light absorption.

Professional Plant Care & Maintenance Protocol

Light Exposure

Position within 1 to 2 meters of an east or south-facing window with sheer curtains. Direct, intense afternoon sun scorches leaves, while deep shade inhibits fenestration splits.

Watering Protocol

Allow the top 5 cm (top 2 inches) of potting soil to dry before watering again. During warm summer growth, irrigation is needed more frequently than during cooler winter dormancy.

Substrate & Soil Mix

Use a chunky, well-draining aroid potting mix composed of 40% coco coir/peat, 25% pine bark nuggets, 20% perlite or pumice, and 15% worm castings for continuous nutrition.

Nutrient & Fertilization

Apply a balanced water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20 or organic seaweed blend) diluted to half strength once every two weeks during spring and summer active leaf production.
